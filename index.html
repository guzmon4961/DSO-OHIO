Master Services & Sublease Agreement Preview

Transparent, Ohio-compliant legal agreement preview for review by your legal counsel.

[PREVIEW] MASTER SERVICES & FACILITY SUBLEASE AGREEMENT

PARTIES: The Streets Are Hungry LLC ("DSO") and [Practice Name], PC/LLC ("Practice").

RECITALS: Practice desires to operate a professional dental clinic at DSO's Central Ohio facility. DSO shall provide facility sublease, billing, payroll, IT, marketing, and non-clinical practice support.

1. CLINICAL AUTONOMY (ORC Chapter 4715): Practice retains 100% exclusive control over all clinical decisions, patient chart custody, diagnostic policies, and licensed clinical staffing.

2. FACILITY SUBLEASE & MAINTENANCE: DSO shall provide the leased premises, maintaining HVAC, plumbing, janitorial services, structural repairs, and non-clinical fixtures.

3. NON-CLINICAL SERVICES: DSO provides Revenue Cycle Management (RCM), marketing campaigns, administrative payroll processing, and merchant processing support.

4. COMPENSATION & FEES: Practice pays DSO a fixed monthly sublease rent plus a Fair Market Value (FMV) non-percentage management fee for administrative services rendered.
